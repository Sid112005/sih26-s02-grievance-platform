How is Priority Score Calculated?

The Hotspot Priority Score (0–100) is an explainable decision-support metric computed deterministically from verified citizen grievances within the cluster:

DimensionWeightCivic Rationale
Density25%Volume of grievances concentrated within the spatial boundary.
Priority / Severity35%Proportion of High & Critical severity complaints reported by citizens.
Recency Velocity15%Recent 7-day complaint influx relative to historical baseline.
Persistence10%Percentage of grievances remaining in Pending / In Progress state.
Semantic Concentration15%Grievances describing identical root causes (shared defect clusters).

Hotspots experiencing rapid escalation (>+50% growth) receive an automated urgency boost to ensure prompt municipal dispatch.
